Connect this device

Refreshing lines and saving bets both go through your GitHub account, so each device needs a GitHub token. You do this once on each device, and it stays connected after that.

  1. Open the new-token page on GitHub. The name, a one-year expiration and the permissions are already filled in.
  2. Under Repository access, choose Only select repositories and pick both cfb-line-shop and cfb-bet-ledger.
  3. Click Generate token, copy it, and paste it here.

The token is saved only in this browser, and it can only reach those two repositories. If you ever want to cut a device off, delete its token on GitHub.

    How to read the board
    Best line
    Each price is the best line for that bet across your books, with the book under it. A better number wins: Iowa +4½ beats +4, Over 44½ beats Over 45½, and Under 45½ beats Under 44½. When the number is the same, the better price wins. If more than one book has the best line, they're all listed, like FD · DK.
    Highlighter
    The books disagree on this bet, and this is the better side of that. No highlight means the books have the same line, so it doesn't matter where you bet it. On a computer, hover over a price to see every book's line.
    Weather
    The forecast at the stadium for kickoff and the next three hours: the temperature, the sky or the highest chance of rain or snow, and the strongest wind. Wind matters most for totals and kicking, so games with 15 mph wind or 25 mph gusts are flagged Windy. Dome means indoors. Hover over the forecast on a computer to see the stadium.
    Middle
    Taking opposite sides at different books so that a narrow band of results wins both bets.
    Arb
    Both sides together pay out more than you stake whatever happens. Rare, and gone quickly when it shows up.
    Your card
    Tap a price to add that bet to your card; tap it again to take it off. After you place it in the sportsbook app, fix the book, odds or stake if they changed and press Add to ledger. Your card is saved on the device you're using.
    Rotation
    The number to the left of each team is the sportsbook rotation number, the same one printed on betting sheets.

    Loading your ledger…

    Your ledger is saved in a private GitHub repository, so only devices you've connected can see it. Changes from your phone and computer show up on both.
